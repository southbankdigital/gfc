Process & Capabilities

Crucible manufacturing is material-heavy, dusty, and heat-driven. Our process is built around practical production control from intake through shipping.

Raw Materials

Refractory base materials are staged for production with attention to lot tracking and moisture control.

Mixing and Material Preparation

Materials are blended for consistency and prepared to support target performance in thermal service.

Forming

Crucibles are formed by mold-based methods and shop equipment suited to the selected geometry and density targets.

Curing and Drying

Formed pieces are stabilized through controlled curing and drying before high-heat firing.

Firing

Firing schedules are used to develop structure and thermal durability for demanding production environments.

Finishing

Edges and surfaces are finished to maintain fit, handling quality, and process readiness.

Inspection

Inspection includes dimensional checks and visual review for consistency and shipping readiness.

Packaging and Shipping

Finished crucibles are packed for transport and staged for regional and national shipment.

Talk Through Your Process Requirements

We can help align product type and size with your furnace and operating range.
