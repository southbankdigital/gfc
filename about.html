About GFY Crucible

GFY stands for Gordon, Fisher & Young Crucible. We are an Ohio-based industrial manufacturer focused on dependable crucible performance for high-heat operations.

Company Overview

GFY Crucible serves foundries, casting shops, heat-processing facilities, and manufacturers that need crucibles capable of handling demanding thermal conditions. Our operation in Hillsboro, Ohio supports practical product selection and dependable supply.

The Gordon, Fisher & Young partnership grew from hands-on foundry and production experience. The company was built around what plant managers and operators ask for most: quality, durability, and consistency.

Quality

Materials and process controls that support dependable in-service performance.
